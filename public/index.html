How CampusFind Works

Three straightforward steps to help reunite lost property with their rightful owners.

01

Report in 60 Seconds

Post item details, upload a photo, specify location (lab, library, canteen) and add your contact details.

02

Instant Search & Filter

Search by category, date, or keywords. Verified students can instantly reach out through secured contact channels.

03

Safe Handover & Close

Meet at campus security or designated block, verify ownership details, and mark the report resolved.
